Mini MSPLC

Mini MSPLC is a compact compiler path used in the development and bootstrap story of Semantic.

Its purpose is to keep a smaller compiler implementation available for experimentation, language validation, and incremental compiler development.

Role in the Project

  • Test and validate language features.
  • Keep compiler experiments small and understandable.
  • Support bootstrap work.
  • Provide a stepping stone toward more compiler components written in Semantic.

Mini MSPLC is therefore closely connected to the self-hosting goal.
